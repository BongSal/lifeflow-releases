Everything for your day, in one place

Lifeflow follows a simple rhythm. Plan what matters, work in focused sessions, take real breaks, then look back at how the day went.

  • Plan
  • Work
  • Take breaks

Today at a glance

One screen shows what's due, what's next, your habits and today's focus time.

Focus and break timer

Focus sessions and breaks run one after another by themselves. You can set focus hours that start the timer on your work days.

Tasks

Add tasks with categories, due dates and repeats, and tick them off as you go.

Calendar and holidays

See your events by week or month. Public holidays for your country are added automatically.

Reminders

Reminders arrive on time, even when the app is closed.

Notes and boards

Write notes in Markdown. Organise projects on Kanban boards with drag and drop.

Habits

Track daily, weekly or monthly habits with streaks and gentle alerts.

Expenses

Record spending in USD or riel and see totals by category.

Statistics

Look back at your focus time, finished tasks and habits for the week.

Private by design

Lifeflow has no accounts and no Lifeflow server. Everything you write stays on your phone or computer unless you choose to back it up.

Read the privacy policy
  • Stored on your device Your tasks, notes and the rest are kept in a database on the device.
  • Sync through your own Google Drive Backups go to a hidden folder in your Drive and keep your phone and computer in sync. Lifeflow can't see any other file in your Drive.
  • English and ខ្មែរ The whole app is available in English and in Khmer.
  • Updates itself New versions are installed from inside the app on Android and desktop.
