Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026

In short: Lifeflow keeps your data on your device. There is no Lifeflow server and no Lifeflow account. If you turn on Google Drive backup, your data goes from your device to your own Google Drive and nowhere else. The app also sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off.

This policy covers the Lifeflow app for Android, Windows, macOS and Linux (“Lifeflow”, “the app”). It explains what data the app handles, where that data goes and the choices you have.

Your data stays on your device

Lifeflow keeps your tasks, calendar events, reminders, notes, boards, habits, expenses, focus-timer history and settings in a database on your phone or computer. The developer receives none of it. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, all of it is deleted unless you made a backup.

Google Drive backup and sync (optional)

You can connect your Google account to back up your data and keep your devices in sync. The app asks for one permission only: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. With it, Lifeflow can create, read and delete its own backup files in a hidden app folder in your Google Drive. It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.

  • Backups are sent straight from your device to Google Drive. They don't pass through any server run by the developer, and the developer can't access them.
  • The app keeps the 10 newest backups and deletes older ones from the same hidden folder.
  • On desktop, the sign-in token that keeps you connected is stored only in the app's database on your computer. On Android, Google Play services handles sign-in.
  • You can disconnect at any time in Settings. To delete the backups as well, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings → Manage apps, find Lifeflow and choose “Delete hidden app data”. You can also remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Lifeflow's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your Google data is used only to back up and sync your own Lifeflow data. It is never sold, never used for advertising and never shared with anyone, and no person reads it.

Anonymous usage statistics

To help improve the app, the Android and desktop apps send anonymous usage statistics to Google Analytics. This is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings → Your data → “Share usage statistics”. These are the only things sent:

  • the name of each screen you open (for example “tasks” or “board”), but never its content, ids or anything you typed
  • when the app is opened and how long it is on screen
  • the app version, the platform (Android or desktop), the app's language, the device category (phone, tablet or desktop), the operating system, and your country as worked out by the app
  • a random id made by the app on your device, so that visits from the same install can be counted together

No name, email address, Google account, device model or hardware id is sent. Nothing from your tasks, notes or other content is sent. Every request tells Google that the data may not be used for advertising or ad personalisation. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy.

Public information the app downloads

Lifeflow downloads some public information. These requests contain none of your data. However, the services can see your device's IP address, as they would for any internet request.

  • Public holidays for the countries you follow, from Nager.Date.
  • Currency exchange rates, from open.er-api.com.
  • In Cambodia, fuel prices published by the Ministry of Commerce, from a public spreadsheet kept by Open Development Cambodia and hosted by Google.
  • Checks for app updates and update downloads, from GitHub.

Device permissions

  • Notifications and alarms: used for reminders, habit alerts, the focus timer and holiday alerts. They are scheduled on your device.
  • Country: on Android, the app reads the country of your SIM card or mobile network to choose public holidays and the local currency. On other devices it uses your time zone. The country stays on your device. The only place it is sent is the anonymous statistics described above.
  • Install updates (Android): lets the app install its own updates after you confirm.

Children

Lifeflow is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
